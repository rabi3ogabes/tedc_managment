# خطة الجلسة — أسئلة صفية فعّالة / Session plan

**Duration / المدة:** 3 hours · **Level:** teachers, all stages

| Time | Activity | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | Warm-up: "worst question you were ever asked" | pairs |
| 15–50 | Chapters 1–2: purposes and Bloom levels | sorting cards (drag questions to levels) |
| 50–90 | Chapter 3: wording | rewrite five weak questions in groups |
| 90–120 | Break and micro-teaching prep | — |
| 120–160 | Chapter 4: wait time, think–pair–share | live demonstration |
| 160–180 | Final assessment, reflection | individual |

**Trainer notes:** collect rewritten questions on the wall; link to the lesson notes in the portal.
