# Business continuity plan

**ملخص:** الأدوار وخطة الاتصال ومصفوفة التصعيد (المستوى الأول والثاني ومدير المشروع والرئيس التنفيذي) واستمرارية الأنشطة التدريبية عند انقطاع المنصة.

## Roles
Incident commander (on-call lead) · communications lead · technical lead · Ministry liaison · scribe.

## Escalation matrix
| Level | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | service desk (Saaed) | user reports, first diagnosis |
| L2 | platform engineers on call | alert fired, > 15 min unresolved |
| PM | project manager | > 1 h or data risk |
| CEO / Ministry lead | executive sponsor | > 4 h, security incident, data exposure |

## Communication
Status message on the internal status page and by e-mail/SMS to training coordinators; update every 30 minutes; final report within 5 working days. A personal-data incident is reported to the Ministry's DPO and the competent authority per Law 13/2016.

## Continuity of training
Sessions continue manually: attendance by the paper sheet and QR fallback (kiosk offline mode), exams by the offline paper variant, certificates issued after recovery with original dates. Offline mobile content (Phase 10) keeps courses available.
