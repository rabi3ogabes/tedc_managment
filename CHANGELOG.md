### 2026-10-15 — Hardening pass: every screen opened as every role, fixes and two features
A Playwright sweep opened all 140 routes as the public visitor and nine demo roles and recorded console errors, failed requests, error boundaries and untranslated keys; the findings below were fixed, with tests.

The answer never reveals whether an account exists, accounts that must use single sign-on get no link, the link follows the password policy shown on the page, and a reset ends the account's other sessions. - **AI diagnosis of an error** (`POST /admin/error-logs/{id}/diagnose`): the model assigned to content work in Settings → AI models (a Claude key, for one) reads the scrubbed entry and answers with the likely cause, fix steps, files, how to verify and a confidence; the answer stays on the entry with a copy button. - **Admission rules**: the sample seeder saved priority criteria as `{key, weight}` objects with unknown names, which broke the page (duplicate React keys, raw labels) and silently disabled prioritisation; the seeder, the service (unknown shapes fall back to the RFP defaults) and the page were corrected.

- **Permission-denied pages**: a page a role may not open (or a switched-off feature) now says so and offers the way back to the person's home page, instead of "unexpected error" with a retry; the trainer, supervisor, kit developer and QA accounts met this on `/admin`.

## 2026-10-14 — Phase 23: enterprise identity and Microsoft 365
Staff can sign in with the Ministry account in three ways that share one provisioning rule, on the web and in the app. **SAML 2.0** (alongside OpenID Connect): TEDC publishes its metadata and signs its requests; the answer is accepted only when the signed assertion is the one used (signature wrapping fails), its audience, recipient, destination, time (±3 minutes), request id and replay register all hold; encrypted assertions and single logout work both ways. The signing key is made and rolled over from the settings (next key published first); the identity provider's metadata is pasted or fetched, shown with every certificate's expiry (a warning 30 days before) and refreshed daily from its address. **In the app** a "Sign in with your Ministry account" button opens the system browser with PKCE and returns a one-time code bound to the app's verifier to `qa.edu.tedc.app://sso`. **Test sign-in** shows administrators the claims, the account and the roles a sign-in would give, without signing anyone in. An existing account is claimed by e-mail only when the provider speaks for its domain or marks the e-mail verified. "Single sign-on mandatory for Ministry staff" hides the password form for staff and keeps the super administrator's break-glass sign-in. **LDAP / Active Directory** is proven against OpenLDAP over LDAPS and STARTTLS in a new CI job (disabled accounts, nested groups to roles, plain LDAP and untrusted certificates refused), the ldap extension is in the image, and `docs/security/identity-methods.md` (Arabic and English) says what the Ministry configures for Kerberos, certificates, FIDO2 and partner mutual TLS. **Teams:** sessions can be **town halls** (Microsoft Graph, created through a connected organiser account, published, invited from the registrations, attendance by time in the event) and TEDC notifications can land in the **Teams activity feed** (a new notification channel with a deep link, plus a downloadable Teams app package); the integration card lists the Graph permissions with a tick for each one the tenant granted. Fixed: a duplicate SAML assertion aborted the surrounding transaction on PostgreSQL; the key's certificate subject depended on the host's OpenSSL defaults.

## 2026-10-13 — Phase 22: exam integrity with a privacy-preserving camera presence check
Remote exams can check that one person is at the screen for the whole exam. The browser counts faces in a Web Worker (MediaPipe, served from TEDC itself) and notices a face turned away; nobody is identified, no video is recorded and no image leaves the device — only the event's name and time. Learners agree first on a clear Arabic and English card, and anyone without a camera sits the exam in the centre or continues with the attempt marked, as the author chooses. Debounced, conservative events become flags that invigilators clear or uphold with a reason (audited), with a minute-by-minute timeline; integrity data is deleted after the retention period (90 days by default). Off by default, per exam, behind the "Exam proctoring" feature; a DPIA note is ready for the data-protection officer. The app sends such exams to a computer. The web app gains unit tests in CI and a browser smoke test with a fake camera.

## 2026-10-13 — Phase 21: offline learning, adaptive practice, the assistant's retrieval, TEDC as an LTI tool
Trainees download a course to the app and learn without a connection: downloads resume, are checked against the server's checksum and kept encrypted (AES-256-GCM, a key for that phone only, in its keystore) until they expire; videos play from a private temporary copy, PDF slides and articles open in the app, and the progress is sent safely when the phone is back online. Exams the trainer allows can be downloaded, taken offline with a timer the phone's clock cannot fool, and handed in later; the server checks the package, the phone and the timing again. A Downloads screen shows the space used, each course's expiry, remove and Wi-Fi only; signing out deletes it all (after a warning when something was not sent). PDF presentations now complete from the slides viewed, each after the author's minimum time, in the app as on the web, and full-screen lessons play only in a landscape full-screen player. Adaptive practice asks one question at a time at the learner's level in each competency and says why each question was chosen (web and app); review lessons are assigned to the learners who need them. The assistant has a pgvector store, model embeddings, streaming answers with Stop and a golden-set evaluation. Other learning platforms can launch TEDC as an LTI 1.3 or 1.1 tool. Fixed: busy pages could make low-limit routes refuse requests (429), tall windows had their top out of reach, learners saw option ids in their result review.
